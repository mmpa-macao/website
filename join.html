    入會申請Membership

    有意加入的人士可以通過填寫下方的線上申請表，或通過其他申請方式成為會員。 Anyone who wishes to join can complete the online application form below or use other application methods.

    入會資格與流程Eligibility and process

    根據本會章程第二章第六條，會員類別、申請資格及入會費如下： According to Article 6 of the Association's constitution, the membership classes, criteria and admission fees are as follows:

    個人會員Medical Physics Member

    入會費 澳門幣 500 元Admission fee MOP 500

    永久性會費Permanent

    年滿二十歲，並同時具備：Aged 20 or above, and holding both:

    • 醫學物理、醫學工程、放射科學、醫學影像或相關學歷；a qualification in medical physics, biomedical engineering, radiological science, medical imaging or a related field;
    • 從事醫學放射專業工作兩年或以上。two or more years of work in a medical radiation profession.

    準會員Associate Member

    入會費 澳門幣 300 元Admission fee MOP 300

    永久性會費Permanent

    訓練與工作年資未達個人會員資格者。For those whose training and work experience do not yet meet the requirements for Medical Physics Membership.

    贊助會員Sponsoring Member

    入會費 澳門幣 2,000 元Admission fee MOP 2,000

    年費：澳門幣 10,000 元/年Plus an annual fee of MOP 10,000

    贊同本會宗旨，並贊助本會經費及工作之團體或個人，經理事會通過者。Organizations or individuals who endorse the Association's objectives and support its funding and work, as approved by the Board.

    詳細可閲讀《澳門醫學物理學會會員入會管理制度》。 Please read the Association's Membership Regulations for details. 下載文件（PDF）Download File (PDF)

    申請流程How it works

    1. 提交申請Submit your application 在下方填寫線上申請表並上傳文件；也可電郵或郵寄提交（見「其他申請方式」）。 Complete the online form below and upload your documents; you can also apply by email or by post (see “Other ways to apply”).
    2. 收件確認及補正Acknowledgement and completion 本會將於 3 個工作日內向您發出收件確認。材料不齊全者，須自通知發出之日起 10 個工作日內補正。 The Secretariat acknowledges receipt within 3 working days. If documents are missing, supply them within 10 working days of the notice.
    3. 繳費及發證Pay and receive your card 結果以電郵或書面形式通知。獲批准者須在通知發出後 15 天內繳費；完成後辦理會員登記，並納入會員名冊。 Results will be notified by email or in writing. If approved, pay within 15 days of the notice ; you are then registered and added to the roll.

    申請前請準備Have these ready

    • 照片；a photo;
    • 身份證明文件副本；a copy of your identity document;
    • 學歷證明文件副本；a copy of your qualification certificate;
    • 工作經歷證明（如適用）；proof of work experience (if applicable);
