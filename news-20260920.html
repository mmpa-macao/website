    澳門醫學物理學會成立典禮 Inauguration Ceremony of the Macau Medical Physics Association

    發佈日期　2026.08.01Posted 2026.08.01

    海報Poster 活動流程Programme

    資訊Details

    • 日期：Date:2026/09/20（日），下午 3:00 ~ 6:00Sunday, 20 September 2026, 3:00 – 6:00 PM
    • 形式：Format:實體In person
    • 地點：Venue:澳門旅遊塔會議中心Macau Tower Convention Centre
    • 費用：Fee:免費Free
    • 主辦單位：Organizer:澳門醫學物理學會Macau Medical Physics Association

    活動簡介About the event

    澳門醫學物理學會（下稱「本會」）於 2024 年 9 月 19 日登記成立。本會謹訂於 2026 年 9 月 20 日（星期日）下午三時正，假澳門旅遊塔會議中心舉行成立典禮。是次活動將特邀北京、澳門及香港三地之醫學物理、臨床醫療、生物醫學工程等領域之專家學者，以及多個相關專業學會代表出席。 The Macau Medical Physics Association (the "Association") was formally registered on 19 September 2024, founded jointly by medical physicists and radiographers working in radiotherapy, medical imaging and nuclear medicine in Macau as a non-profit academic organization. Its mission is to advance the professional development of medical physics in Macau, promote cross-regional academic exchange, cultivate professional talent, encourage more young people in Macau to pursue careers in medical physics, and deepen collaboration with related disciplines. The inaugural ceremony will be held on Sunday, 20 September 2026 at 3:00 PM, at the Macau Tower Convention Centre, with invited experts and scholars from Beijing, Macau and Hong Kong in medical physics, clinical medicine and biomedical engineering, alongside representatives of related professional societies. Approximately 100 attendees are expected.
