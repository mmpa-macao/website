格物致知，學理彰醫 Explore nature to know, master physics to heal

澳門醫學物理學會致力於推廣醫學物理的應用與研究，並普及輻射防護與安全知識。 我們面向醫學物理、放射科學、醫學工程等領域的從業人員及學習者，透過舉辦學術研討、繼續教育培訓、刊物出版與對外交流，同時提供輻射安全諮詢，持續提升本地專業水平與服務質量。 The Macau Medical Physics Association advances research and application in medical physics while furthering public and professional understanding of radiation safety. We serve clinicians, engineers, researchers, and trainees across medical physics, radiology, and allied health fields. Through academic symposia, continuing education, professional publications, cross-border collaborations, and radiation safety consulting, the Association supports high standards of practice throughout Macao.
