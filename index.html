    成立典禮Inauguration ceremony

    2026 年 9 月 20 日，澳門醫學物理學會舉行成立典禮。 The Association's inauguration ceremony was held on 20 September 2026.

    專業介紹及升學講座Professional Introduction and Higher Education Seminar

    面向中學生舉辦升學講座，吸引更多對數學、物理、醫學有興趣的學生投身醫學物理相關領域。 Deliver higher education talks at secondary schools, to encourage students passionate about mathematics, physics, and medicine to enter the field of medical physics.

    學術座談Academic forums

    舉辦學術座談，推動本地及跨地區的學術研討與交流。 Hold academic forums that foster academic discussion and exchange, locally and across regions.

    格物致知，學理彰醫 Explore nature to know, master physics to heal

    本會屬非牟利社團，致力於推廣醫學物理的應用與研究，並普及輻射防護與安全知識。 我們面向醫學物理、放射科學、醫學工程等領域的從業人員及學習者，透過舉辦學術研討、繼續教育培訓、刊物出版與對外交流，同時提供輻射安全諮詢，持續提升本地專業水平與服務質量。 The Macao Medical Physics Association is a non-profit organization that advances research and application in medical physics while furthering public and professional understanding of radiation safety. We serve clinicians, engineers, researchers, and trainees across medical physics, radiology, and allied health fields. Through academic symposia, continuing education, professional publications, cross-border collaborations, and radiation safety consulting, the Association supports high standards of practice throughout Macao.
