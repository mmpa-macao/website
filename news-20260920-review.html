    澳門醫學物理學會成立典禮｜活動回顧 Inauguration Ceremony of the Macao Medical Physics Association — Event Review

    發佈日期　2026.10.22Posted 2026.10.22

    澳門醫學物理學會成立典禮於 2026 年 9 月 20 日（星期日）下午假澳門旅遊塔會議中心舉行，來自北京、澳門及香港三地的專家學者與相關專業學會代表出席。本會於 2024 年 9 月 19 日登記成立，致力推動澳門醫學物理專業的發展，促進跨地區學術交流，培養專業人才，並鼓勵更多澳門青年投身醫學物理行業。 The inauguration ceremony of the Macao Medical Physics Association was held on the afternoon of Sunday, 20 September 2026 at the Macau Tower Convention Centre, attended by experts, scholars and representatives of related societies from Beijing, Macau and Hong Kong. Registered on 19 September 2024, the Association was founded as a non-profit academic organization, aiming to advance the profession of medical physics in Macau, promote cross-regional academic exchange, nurture talent and encourage young people in Macau to pursue careers in the field..

    成立剪綵儀式由仁伯爵綜合醫院代院長曾潭飛、衛生局醫務活動牌照處代處長黎冠豪、澳門離島醫療綜合體北京協和醫院澳門醫學中心內科部主任冷曉梅、本會會長李敏兒、本會臨床顧問兼仁伯爵綜合醫院放射治療專科醫生梁頌文、鏡湖醫院腫瘤科副主任曹亞兵、香港醫學物理學院主席張健賢、香港醫學物理學會會長李嘉豪，以及本會副會長廖偉霆共同主持。 The programme included a ribbon-cutting ceremony officiated by representatives of the Macau Health Bureau and local hospitals, medical physics bodies from Hong Kong, and officers of the Association; the presentation of appointment certificates to four advisors (clinical, academic, association affairs and financial); academic talks on the Association's work plan and on the development of medical physics in Macau and in Mainland China; and a panel discussion.

    會長李敏兒致辭時指出，醫學物理是連接物理學與臨床醫學的橋樑學科。過去澳門的從業者分散在不同機構，缺少專屬的交流平台，學會經約一年半籌備後成立，期望填補本澳相關專業團體的空白。 In her address, the President described medical physics as a bridge between physics and clinical medicine, and noted that practitioners in Macau had long worked separately without a dedicated platform. The Association was established, after about a year and a half of preparation, to fill that gap.

    典禮上，本會向梁頌文（臨床顧問）、莫昇萍教授（學術顧問）、鄭美鈺（會務顧問）及林珊珊（財務顧問）頒發聘狀。 Appointment certificates were presented to the Association's clinical, academic, association affairs and financial advisors.

    在學術分享環節中，會長李敏兒率先以澳門醫學物理的發展與現狀為題作分享；隨後，特別邀請北京協和醫院放射治療科汪之群醫學物理師介紹國內醫學物理的最新發展與行業動態；最後，由副會長廖偉霆報告本會的工作計畫及會刊籌備進展。 During the academic session, President Ms. Li Miner delivered an opening presentation on the development and current status of medical physics in Macau. Mr. Wang Zhiqun, a medical physicist from the Department of Radiation Oncology at Peking Union Medical College Hospital, was then invited to share insights into the industry trends in Mainland China. Finally, Vice-President Mr. Liao Weiting presented the Association’s work plan and the updates on its upcoming bulletin.

    座談環節由莫昇萍教授主持，與談嘉賓包括香港醫學物理學院主席張健賢博士、香港醫學物理學會會長李嘉豪博士、香港大學李嘉誠醫學院名譽副教授鄭致遠博士、楊彬、汪之群，以及本會會長李敏兒。與會專家圍繞醫學物理專業在本澳的發展方向、人才培育及公眾科普等議題交流，期望為醫學物理與相關領域建立更緊密的合作網絡。 The panel discussion brought together experts from Macau, Hong Kong and Beijing to exchange views on the future development of medical physics in Macau, talent development and public outreach, with the aim of building a closer network of cooperation between medical physics and related fields.

    出席嘉賓還包括澳門放射師學會、澳門物理學會、香港放射治療學會及香港東華學院等團體和院校的代表。本會感謝各位撥冗出席，並將以成立典禮為新的起點，繼續推動澳門醫學物理專業的發展。 Representatives of other professional societies and institutions from Macau and Hong Kong also attended. The Association would like to express sincere gratitude to all attendees. Moving forward, the Association will continue to promote the development of medical physics in Macau.
